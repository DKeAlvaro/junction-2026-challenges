# Vela: submission text

## Project name
Vela

## Punchline
Vela is the AI companion for elderly people. She lives in your Meta glasses and quietly tells you the names of the people you love, so forgetting never keeps you from living on your own.

## Description
Vela is the AI companion for elderly people.

Many people over 70 want to keep living on their own, but names start to slip. People with early signs of Alzheimer's forget the names of the people they love the most, and so they stop going out and stop answering the door.

Vela lives inside your Meta glasses and reminds you who you are seeing: your niece, your uncle, your neighbour, your doctor. When someone you know comes into view, she says it softly in your ear: *"That's Diego, your grandson."* If she doesn't know the person, she stays silent.

Teaching her takes one sentence: *"Vela, this is Pedro, my neighbour."* It works by voice, in English or Spanish, with nothing new to learn.

**How it works.** The glasses send a photo every few seconds. The phone app finds and compares faces on the device, so photos never leave the phone. Only text and sound go online (Whisper for speech, a small LLM for name and relationship, ElevenLabs for Vela's voice). She speaks once per person every 10 minutes.

**Privacy.** Vela only recognises people who have been introduced to her, and the wearer decides who she remembers.

**Built:** a working PWA and an Android shell for Ray-Ban Meta glasses (Wearables Device Access Toolkit). **Next:** the same loop can catch scams, check pills against the schedule and recap doctor visits.

**Who pays:** the wearer, with the glasses once plus a small monthly subscription. Later, insurers and telecare services can subsidise it.
