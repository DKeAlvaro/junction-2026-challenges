# Healthcare track

## AstraZeneca — Equal Care Across Finland

### Summary
Help ensure equitable and effective cancer care for everyone in Finland. Identify where the cancer care pathway can be strengthened – is it through earlier detection, smoother referrals between health centers and hospitals, or faster treatment initiation? Develop a nationwide, AI-powered solution to support equal and effective cancer care across Finland.

### Description
At AstraZeneca stand we will provide key insights to the challenge on Friday at 20:00

**Introduction:** AstraZeneca is inviting you to explore where cancer care pathways can be strengthened and identify a solution that could improve equal cancer care across Finland.

**The Challenge:** The challenge is ensuring equal and effective cancer care in a rapidly changing environment. Ensure that time to diagnosis is optimal and patients start treatment within target timeframes, wherever they live in Finland. Build an AI-powered system that supports equal and effective cancer care across Finland. Your solution should:

- Identify the gap. Detect differences in access to cancer care or treatment outcomes.
- Locate the gap in the care pathway. Show where the difference emerges: detection, referral from primary to specialist care, diagnosis, or treatment initiation.
- Recommend what to do. This is the most important part. Provide concrete, evidence-based improvement proposals that could be implemented at national or account level across Finland. Diagnosing the problem is not enough. Show how it could be addressed.

**Possible Solutions:** The format is open. Possible solutions include:

- An AI analyst that explains indicator trends and proposes actions.
- A monitoring dashboard with recommendations.
- An early-warning system for emerging delays.
- A simulation showing the potential impact of an intervention.

**Minimum Requirements:**

- Use reliable evidence. Base your findings on public data and/or insights gathered from AstraZeneca mentors. Clearly show the sources supporting every claim.
- Address Goal 3. Cover at least one indicator connected to Goal 3 of the national cancer strategy.
- Provide actionable recommendations. Deliver concrete improvement proposals, not just an analysis.
- Demonstrate a working solution. Submit a working demo. Slides alone are not sufficient.

## Aalto University — Beyond the Clinic

### Summary
Build the product that helps people stay well at home after depression treatment, using anything from natural stimuli to a phone, music, games, light or AI.

### Description
**Introduction:** Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) is used in hospitals and clinics to treat people with severe depression. The treatment usually requires multiple brief daily visits for brain stimulation. Even if the treatment is successful, there is a risk that depression returns. Therefore, we would like to have “maintenance” treatment at home.

**The Challenge:** In the hospital, you can obtain blood samples, get brain scans with fMRI, MEG or EEG, and be consulted by a psychiatrist. At home you don’t have these, except for an occasional remote psychotherapy meeting, and that is also difficult or expensive. But brain activity and mental condition at home can be measured in several ways. No good solution has yet been developed for maintenance treatment at home, and that is the challenge. Home-based maintenance treatment could also be “closed-loop”, meaning that it is individualised based on how the brain of the patient reacts to the interventions. In other words, your product can work as a loop: sense how someone is doing, respond with something that helps, and learn what works best for that person.
