# Vela: launch video

*Draft 2, 13 November 2026. One version, replaces the five variations of draft 1. Follows `vela-product-vision.md` (Ray-Ban Meta Gen2, voice only, phone as digital twin).*

**The idea.** One day of Elena's life, seen through her eyes. Vela speaks three times, then the film shows how it works and how it reaches people. Everything except three short glasses shots can be filmed today on a phone. Tonight we build the whole edit with placeholders in those three slots. Once the prototype runs on the glasses, we film the slots, drop them in, and export.

**Length:** 1:50. Hard cap 1:55 (Junction allows 2:00 at most).

---

## 1. Rules

- **Submission:** public on YouTube, linked in the submission before **Sun 15.11 10:00**. English, subtitles burned in.
- **Real footage comes early.** The real prototype is on screen by 0:28, not saved for the end. Last year's finalists who kept the demo for last, or passed off a concept video as the product, lost points for it (`research/junction-2025-finals.md`).
- **Honesty:**
  - Real prototype shots carry a corner tag: `Real prototype · unedited`. Everything else is staged, and the end card says so.
  - Wording: Vela "points out", "confirms", "reminds". Never "prevents", "manages", "elderly".
- **No screen on the glasses.** Vela speaks in the ear, so the film never draws text on a "lens". Her lines appear only as subtitles. Say it once on screen: *No screen. Just her voice.*
- **Firefly:** the only on-screen light in the story is a small amber dot before Vela's subtitles. It glows while she speaks. It also shows up in the wordmark.
- **Look:**
  - Colours: amber `#FFB54A`, near-black `#0D0B09`, cream `#F3EBDD`.
  - Font: Atkinson Hyperlegible for subtitles and text, a humanist serif for the wordmark.
  - Subtitles: Vela's lines in amber, everyone else in cream.
  - Grade: warm, lit by 2700 K lamps. The hook is the one desaturated part.
- **Sound:**
  - Every Vela line is preceded by the same soft chime the app plays.
  - Music: one soft piano track. It starts at the title, dips under speech, and swells at the end card. Use a free licence (YouTube Audio Library or Pixabay Music).

## 4. Shoot today

Roughly 2 hours, grouped by setup. Shoot 3 takes of everything. Leave 2 s of stillness before and after each take.

**A. Elena's kitchen.** A dressed table at the venue: tablecloth, mug, plant, warm lamp, a radio, the pill organiser. POV only shows what is in front of the hands, so this reads as a home.
- Scene 3: the pill, POV. Wear the amber cardigan sleeve and the ring.
- Scene 4a: envelope on a mat by a door, picked up, then opened at the table.
- `G1` placeholder: pick up and put down, with stand-in glasses.
- Hook inserts: pill box close-up, letter on the doormat. For the dark step, use a stairwell with the lights low.
- When done, photograph the table from three angles and bag the props. `G2` must match this set.

**B. Doctor.** A desk with a laptop and some papers. The teammate playing Dr. Laine wears a shirt. Film POV from the patient's chair, with the consent line said clearly.

**C. Outside.** 10 s of walking POV outside the venue, at dusk if possible.

**D. Ana.**
- Office desk: a laptop and a coffee, the phone face up. Call it from another phone, saved under the contact name *Mum*.
- Sofa in dim light: the phone shows the family app, and a thumb scrolls.
- Also screen-record the app on its own, for a clean insert.

## 5. Voices

- **Vela.** Pick the voice tonight and use the same one in the app, so the film and the product sound alike. Calm, warm, unhurried. Generate these lines now, plus a TTS backup of the `G2` and `G3` lines:
  1. "Good morning, Elena."
  2. "The blue one is done for today. The white one is next."
  3. "This isn't from your bank. They never ask you to call a new number. Shall I call Ana?"
  4. "Calling Ana."
  5. "One change today: the white pill moves to the evening. I've sent Ana the summary."
  6. "I've lost the connection. I'll tell you when I'm back." / "I'm back."
  7. "Good night, Elena."
- **Elena.** Four short lines: "Vela, which one now?", "Vela, what's this?", "Yes, please.", "Please." Best voiced by an older relative as a voice note (with their OK). Otherwise a teammate, soft and slow. In the real `G2` take, whoever wears the glasses speaks. That's fine, because the take is tagged.
- **Ana and Dr. Laine.** Teammates. Record their lines on set or as voice memos.
- **Team V.O. (scene 8).** One teammate, about 20 s. Record in a quiet small room; a blanket over the head works.

## 6. Edit

- Master: 16:9, 1080p or 4K, 25 fps.
  - POV shots fill the frame.
  - `G2` uses two portrait panels side by side; together they fill 16:9 almost exactly.
- Build these once tonight:
  - subtitle styles (cream; amber with the dot)
  - the split-screen template with tag and timer
  - the three-step diagram
  - title and end cards
  - the tag `Real prototype · unedited`
- Family app mock: a single static HTML page in the brand colours, opened on a phone and screen-recorded. Swap in the real app if it exists by Saturday.
- Tool: whatever the editor knows best (DaVinci Resolve or CapCut).

## 7. Props

- **Scam letter.** From an invented bank, e.g. "Kestrel Savings"; check it doesn't resemble a real one. Print two copies and keep one clean for `G2`.
- **Pills.** A pill organiser with blue and white mints.
- **Elena.** An amber cardigan and a ring.
- **Kitchen set.** Tablecloth, mug, plant, warm lamp, radio, a doormat.
- **Stand-in glasses.** Wayfarer-style, for the `G1` placeholder.
- **Kit.** Two phones, a tripod or a phone head strap for POV.
- **Releases.** Signed by everyone on camera.
