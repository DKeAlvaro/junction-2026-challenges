# Vela: launch video

*Draft 3, 13 November 2026. One version, made without a camera. Follows `vela-product-vision.md` (Ray-Ban Meta Gen2, voice only, phone as digital twin).*

**The idea.** One day of Elena's life, seen through her eyes. Vela speaks three times, then the film shows how it works and how it reaches people.

**How it's made.** The story is built entirely from stock footage, motion graphics, screen captures and TTS voices, so nobody acts and no camera is needed. The only footage we shoot ourselves is three short glasses shots. They sit in the timeline as placeholders from tonight, and we drop them in once the prototype runs on the glasses.

**Length:** 1:50. Hard cap 1:55 (Junction allows 2:00 at most).

---

## 1. Rules

**Submission.** Public on YouTube, linked in the submission before **Sun 15.11 10:00**. English, subtitles burned in.

**Real footage comes early.** The real prototype is on screen by 0:28, not saved for the end. Last year, finalists who left the demo for last, or passed a concept video off as the product, paid for it (`research/junction-2025-finals.md`).

**Honesty.**
- Real prototype shots carry a corner tag: `Real prototype · unedited`.
- The end card says: *Dramatised with stock footage. Real prototype footage is marked.*
- Wording: Vela "points out", "confirms", "reminds". Never "prevents", "manages", "elderly".

**Stock.**
- **No lip-sync, ever.** All dialogue is voice-over, heard over hands, backs, profiles, objects, or a phone held to the ear.
- **One creator series per character.** On Pexels, open a clip you like and click the creator. They often have 10 to 30 clips of the same model in the same flat. Choose Elena's series first and fill the gaps afterwards. Do the same for Ana.
- **What to avoid:** pensioners smiling at the camera, medical blue, hospital corridors.

**No screen on the glasses.**
- Vela speaks in the ear, so her lines appear only as subtitles. The film never draws text on a lens.
- Say it once on screen: *No screen. Just her voice.*
- The firefly is a small amber dot in front of Vela's subtitles. It glows while she speaks.

**Look.**
- Colours: amber `#FFB54A`, near-black `#0D0B09`, cream `#F3EBDD`.
- Type: Atkinson Hyperlegible for subtitles and text, a humanist serif for the wordmark.
- Subtitles: Vela in amber, everyone else in cream.
- Grade: one warm grade plus light film grain over everything, so clips from different sources read as one film. The hook is the only desaturated part.

**Sound.**
- The same soft chime as the app comes before every Vela line.
- One soft piano track (Pixabay Music or YouTube Audio Library). It starts at the title, dips under speech and swells at the end card.

## 3. Glasses slots

These are the only shots we film ourselves. Each slot sits in the timeline at its final length, with a placeholder in it from tonight. All three fit in one 45-minute session, once the prototype runs on the glasses.

### G1: product shots (scene 2 morning, scene 9 night)

- **Final shot:** the glasses alone on a table, with no hands and no people, so nothing has to match the stock Elena. Two versions: morning light with a slow slide; night by lamp light, locked off.
- **Placeholder tonight:** a Meta press photo of Gen2 with a slow push-in.
- **Notes:** use a wooden table, a mug and a warm lamp, so it matches the stock kitchen.

### G2: the real scam-letter take (scene 4b)

- **Final shot:** two recordings started together and synced on a clap:
  - the phone's screen recording of the Vela app
  - a second phone filming the wearer from the side, in portrait
- **Placeholder tonight:** the same layout, built like this:
  - left: the 4a letter insert
  - right: a card that says *Real take goes here*
  - Vela's line from TTS
- **Notes:**
  - One continuous take: no cuts, no speed-up. The timer starts at the clap.
  - Shoot 10 or more takes and keep the cleanest.
  - Print the letter from the same file as the 4a insert.

### G3: connection test (scene 8)

- **Final shot:** Wi-Fi off, Vela says she has lost the connection. Wi-Fi on, she says she's back. Record it with the screen recording plus the side camera.
- **Placeholder tonight:** a text card.
- **Notes:** it runs 6 s in the edit.

### Before and after the shoot

- **Vela's voice must be audible in the recording.** It comes from the glasses' open-ear speakers. Check this on the first test. If it's too quiet, move the side camera closer, or switch on internal audio capture in the screen recording.
- **If the glasses haven't arrived by Sat 18:00:**
  - `G2` and `G3`: screen-record the real app running on the phone, as one full-width panel tagged `Real prototype · running on the phone`.
  - `G1`: the press photo stays, with a credit. Never show ordinary glasses as the product.

### AI video, only to fill gaps

Use it only when stock can't supply a hands-and-objects shot, for example a hand pausing over one pill compartment.

- **Services:** Sora 2 with `OPENAI_API_KEY`, or Veo with `GEMINI_API_KEY`.
- **Cap:** 12 generations in total, 8 s each. Check the price per second first.
- **Never generate:** faces, glasses, or readable text. Text is always `GFX`.
- **Prompt template:** "Close-up, warm 2700 K kitchen lamp light, an older woman's wrinkled hands with a ring and an amber cardigan sleeve, [action], shallow depth of field, gentle handheld, natural, 35 mm, no faces, no text."
- Match the result to the chosen stock series, then pass it through the same grade and grain.
